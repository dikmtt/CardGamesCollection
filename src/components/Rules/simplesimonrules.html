Overview

Simple Simon is a 1-deck patience game similar to Spider.

Basics

To win Simple Simon, you need to play all cards into the 4 foundation piles. Cards cannot be moved to the foundations by themselves, they must be moved as a stack. A stack can be considered complete and ready to be moved to the foundation if it is a descending run from King to Ace, all in the same suit.

Setup

Deal 10 columns of face up cards. Start by dealing one card to each column, and track the last column where a card was dealt. When dealing the next row, skip dealing cards to that column and all columns to the right of it. When finished, the tableau will have these number of cards in each column:

8 8 8 7 6 5 4 3 2 1

The Play

Cards can be moved to another column if the card is one rank lower than column's top card. Additionally, stacks of cards can be moved if each card differs 1 in rank than the previous one, and all cards in the stack are the same suit. Descending stacks of different suits cannot be moved. 2 examples:

  1. A 2 of Clubs can be moved to a 3 of Spades, but you cannot move both cards together.
  2. A 6 of Hearts can be moved to a 7 of Hearts, and you can move both cards to an available 8.
Once a suited run of cards from King to Ace is made, you can move it to a foundation pile.

Interactions

  • Click on a card or stack to select it. Click on a different card to move it. If it is a valid play, the card or stack will move to the target column.
  • In this implementation, foundation piles are off-screen and complete suited runs of cards are removed from the board entirely. The number of finished stacks are tracked on top.
  • Click the "UNDO" button to undo your last move.

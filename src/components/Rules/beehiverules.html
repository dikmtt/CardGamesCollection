Overview

Beehive is a solitaire game in which the objective is to collect 13 "books" (a.k.a four-of-a-kinds.)

Basics

The object of Beehive is to remove all cards from the play area by playing 4 cards of the same rank on each tableau space. There are 2 ways to play this game. This implementation is a more basic, yet strategic version in which any card may occupy an empty spot. Other versions may force the reserve's top card to occupy any empty spaces, if it still has cards on it.

Setup

Deal a 13-card reserve and a tableau consisting of 6 cards. The rest of the cards become the stock pile.

The Play

You can move a card on top of another card if both cards are the same rank. Cards from the reserve or the waste pile can be played to the tableau following the previous rule. Any card may occupy an empty space. If you are stuck, click on the stock pile to draw cards 3 at a time to the waste. The top card of the waste is playable to the tableau. You are allowed infinite redeals.

When a pile contains all 4 cards of the same rank, it is removed from the game. You win if all 13 4-card "books" are removed from the game, resulting in an empty tableau, waste, and reserve.

Interactions

  • Click on a card to select it. Click on a tableau pile to move that card to the tableau space. If it is a valid play, the card or stack will move to the target column. The game keeps track of how many cards occupy each tableau spot. If any tableu space reaches 4 cards, it is automatically emptied.
  • Click on the stock pile to deal cards 3 at a time to the waste. If it is empty, click on the symbol that appears to redeal. You are allowed infinite redeals.

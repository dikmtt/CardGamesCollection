Overview

This is a draw and discard game also known as 31/41 and Scat. The main implementation here is 41, which uses a 4-card hand.

Basics

The object of the game is to get your score as close to 41 as possible. A hand is valid only if all cards in that hand are the same suit. If two or more suits are present, the suits with the lesser values are deducted from the highest suit's value.

Setup

Deal 4 cards to each player. The remaining deck beomes the stock, and the top card is turned up to form the discard pile.

The Play

The player left of the dealer begins the first play. Players may take from either the stock or the discard pile. Then, that player discards a card from their hand. If they took a card from the discard, they may not discard that same card.

Players build their hand in order to be the closest to 41 using same-suited cards. If a player is confident they will win (their hand must consist of cards of the same suit) they may go out. Alternatively, players can only go out if their score is above a minimum threshold. The latter is implemented here, and the minimum score is 39.

Once a player goes out, every player calculates their hand value. Numbered cards are worth their face value. Face cards are all worth 10, and Aces are 11. Any cards of a different suit will have their value deducted from their score. The player with the lowest score loses.

If a player goes out and another player has a higher hand value than them, they lose the game.

Here are some hand scoring examples:

  • A hand of 3-H, 6-H, 7-H, and J-H has 26 points.
  • A hand of 8-C, 10-C, Q-C, and A-C has 39 points.
  • A hand of 8-C, 10-C, A-C, and 5-S has 24 points. All clubs are worth 29, minus the 5-valued spade.
  • A hand of 10-H, K-H, J-D, and 3-S has 7 points. 20 from Hearts minus 10 from Diamonds and 3 from Spades. The base suit of this hand is Hearts because it has the highest card value.

Interactions

  • If it is your turn, click on either the waste pile or the stock pile to draw a card from there. Then click on a card from your hand to discard it. If any player's score reaches 39 or higher, the game automatically ends. If the stock pile is empty and no one has reached 39, the game also ends automatically.

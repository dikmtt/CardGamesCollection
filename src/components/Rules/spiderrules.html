Overview

Spider Solitaire is a 2-deck patience game. First invented in the 1940s, it became popular after Microsoft shipped it into Windows operating systems starting from Windows 98.

Basics

To win Spider Solitaire, you need to play all cards into the 8 foundation piles. Cards cannot be moved to the foundations by themselves, they must be moved as a stack. A stack can be considered complete and ready to be moved to the foundation if it is a descending run from King to Ace, all in the same suit.

Setup

Deal 10 column of face down cards until there are 5 cards in each column. Then add a card to the first 4 columns. Turn the top card of each stack face up. The rest of the deck becomes the stock, which has 50 cards.

The Play

Cards can be moved to another column if the card is one rank lower than column's top card. Additionally, stacks of cards can be moved if each card differs 1 in rank than the previous one, and all cards in the stack are the same suit. Descending stacks of different suits cannot be moved. 2 examples:

  1. A 2 of Clubs can be moved to a 3 of Spades, but you cannot move both cards together.
  2. A 6 of Hearts can be moved to a 7 of Hearts, and you can move both cards to an available 8.
Once a suited run of cards from King to Ace is made, you can move it to a foundation pile. A face-down card gets turned face up if there are no more cards on top of it. When you can't or don't want to make any more moves, deal a new set of cards from the stock by placing one face-up card to each column. This can only be done five times, after that the stock becomes empty and you can no longer deal more cards.

Difficulties

There are 3 difficulties:

  1. Easy Mode: All cards are the same suit
  2. Medium Mode: Only 2 suits are used
  3. Hard Mode: All 4 suits are used

Interactions

  • Click on a card or stack to select it. Click on a different card to move it. If it is a valid play, the card or stack will move to the target column.
  • In this implementation, foundation piles are off-screen and complete suited runs of cards are removed from the board entirely. The number of finished stacks are tracked on top.
  • Click the "DEAL" button to deal a new set of cards to each column. This can only be done 5 times.

Overview

Sultan Solitaire is a 2-deck patience game. Also called Sultan of Turkey and Emperor of Germany, this is a picturesque solitaire game where you aim to surround the Sultan with a harem of Queens.

Basics

To win Sultan Solitaire, you need to play all cards into the 9 foundation piles. Foundation piles build up in the same suit starting from King or Ace up to Queen. There are 8 reserve piles, each holding 1 card each and no more. There's also a stock and waste pile.

Sultan starts with 9 foundation piles, 8 starting with Kings and one starting with an Ace. The middle card is a King of Hearts, representing the Sultan. The other 8 piles are the foundations that you must build up to Queens. At the end of the game, you will see the Sultan being surrounded by 8 Queens.

Setup

Remove all Kings and one Ace of Hearts from 2 decks of cards and place the cards in a 3x3 grid, making sure one King of Hearts is in the center. Deal 8 cards to represent the reserve pile, and the remaining deck is formed as a stock pile.

The Play

You can play a card to a foundation pile from the waste or a reserve pile if the suits are the same and the rank is one higher than the foundation pile's top card. Reserve piles can only hold 1 card each, and if empty, are immediately filled from the waste pile or the stock if the waste is empty. Click on the stock to deal one card to the waste. If the stock is empty, you can redeal a maximum of 2 times. If there are still cards on the reserve or waste after 2 redeals, the game is lost.

Interactions

  • Click on a card on the reserve or waste to select it. Click on a foundation pile to play the card there. The game will tell you if the play is valid or not. If it is, the card is moved to the foundation pile.
  • Click on the stock pile to deal a card to the waste. If it is empty, click on it to redeal the cards from the waste, this can only be done twice.
